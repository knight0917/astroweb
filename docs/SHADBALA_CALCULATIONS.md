# Classical Parashari Shadbala (षड्बल) Mathematical Reference & Formulas
**Source Text Reference:** *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (BPHS), Chapters 27-29: Shadbala-adhyaya (षड्बलाध्याय)*

Shadbala (षड्बल) is the supreme 6-fold planetary strength assessment system in Vedic Astrology. It quantitatively determines the exact capacity of the 7 Classical Grahas (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) to manifest their results in a native's life.

---

## 1. Units of Measurement
- **1 Rupa (रूप)** = $60 \text{ Shashtiamshas / Virupas (षष्ट्यंश / विरूपा)}$
- All sub-strengths are calculated in **Virupas** and summed into the **Shadbala Pinda (षड्बल पिण्ड)**.

---

## 2. The Six Primary Balas (षड्बल)

### I. Sthana Bala (स्थान बल — Positional Strength)
Sthana Bala measures the strength derived from the planet's spatial position in the zodiac.
$$\text{Sthana Bala} = \text{Uchcha Bala} + \text{Saptavargaja Bala} + \text{Ojayugma Bala} + \text{Kendra Bala} + \text{Drekkana Bala}$$

1. **Uchcha Bala (उच्च बल — Exaltation Strength, Max 60 Virupas):**
   - Deep Exaltation (Paramochcha) vs Deep Debilitation (Paramaneecha):
     - **Sun**: Exaltation $10^\circ$ Aries ($10^\circ$), Debilitation $10^\circ$ Libra ($190^\circ$)
     - **Moon**: Exaltation $3^\circ$ Taurus ($33^\circ$), Debilitation $3^\circ$ Scorpio ($213^\circ$)
     - **Mars**: Exaltation $28^\circ$ Capricorn ($298^\circ$), Debilitation $28^\circ$ Cancer ($118^\circ$)
     - **Mercury**: Exaltation $15^\circ$ Virgo ($165^\circ$), Debilitation $15^\circ$ Pisces ($345^\circ$)
     - **Jupiter**: Exaltation $5^\circ$ Cancer ($95^\circ$), Debilitation $5^\circ$ Capricorn ($275^\circ$)
     - **Venus**: Exaltation $27^\circ$ Pisces ($357^\circ$), Debilitation $27^\circ$ Virgo ($177^\circ$)
     - **Saturn**: Exaltation $20^\circ$ Libra ($200^\circ$), Debilitation $20^\circ$ Aries ($20^\circ$)
   - Formula:
     $$\Delta = |\lambda - \text{NeechaLon}|$$
     $$\text{If } \Delta > 180^\circ \implies \Delta = 360^\circ - \Delta$$
     $$\text{Uchcha Bala} = \frac{\Delta}{3} \quad (\text{Range: } 0 \text{ to } 60 \text{ Virupas})$$

2. **Saptavargaja Bala (सप्तवर्गज बल — 7 Divisional Dignity Strength):**
   - Computed across 7 Vargas: **D1 (Rashi), D2 (Hora), D3 (Drekkana), D7 (Saptamsha), D9 (Navamsha), D12 (Dwadashamsha), D30 (Trimshamsha)**.
   - In each varga:
     - Moolatrikona = $45 \text{ Virupas}$
     - Own Sign (Swakshetra) = $30 \text{ Virupas}$
     - Great Friend (Adhi-Mitra) = $20 \text{ Virupas}$
     - Friend (Mitra) = $15 \text{ Virupas}$
     - Neutral (Sama) = $10 \text{ Virupas}$
     - Enemy (Shatru) = $4 \text{ Virupas}$
     - Great Enemy (Adhi-Shatru) = $2 \text{ Virupas}$

3. **Ojayugma Bala (ओजयुग्म बल — Odd/Even Sign Strength, Max 30 Virupas):**
   - Male planets (Sun, Mars, Jupiter) + Mercury get $15 \text{ Virupas}$ in Odd signs (D1) and $15 \text{ Virupas}$ in Odd navamshas (D9).
   - Female planets (Moon, Venus) + Saturn get $15 \text{ Virupas}$ in Even signs (D1) and $15 \text{ Virupas}$ in Even navamshas (D9).

4. **Kendra Bala (केन्द्र बल — Angular House Strength):**
   - Planet in Kendra House (1, 4, 7, 10) = $60 \text{ Virupas}$
   - Planet in Panapara House (2, 5, 8, 11) = $30 \text{ Virupas}$
   - Planet in Apoklima House (3, 6, 9, 12) = $15 \text{ Virupas}$

5. **Drekkana Bala (द्रेष्काण बल — Decanate Strength):**
   - 1st Decanate ($0^\circ-10^\circ$): Male planets (Sun, Mars, Jupiter) = $15 \text{ Virupas}$
   - 2nd Decanate ($10^\circ-20^\circ$): Hermaphrodite planets (Mercury, Saturn) = $15 \text{ Virupas}$
   - 3rd Decanate ($20^\circ-30^\circ$): Female planets (Moon, Venus) = $15 \text{ Virupas}$

### II. Dig Bala (दिग्बल — Directional Strength, Max 60 Virupas)
- Planets attain maximum Dig Bala in their cardinal house apex (Zero Loss Point):
  - **Jupiter & Mercury** $\to$ East / 1st House (Lagna, $0^\circ$)
  - **Sun & Mars** $\to$ South / 10th House (Midheaven, $270^\circ$ / MC)
  - **Saturn** $\to$ West / 7th House (Descendant, $180^\circ$)
  - **Moon & Venus** $\to$ North / 4th House (Nadir, $90^\circ$ / IC)
- Formula:
  $$\text{Angular Distance } d = |\text{PlanetHouseCusp} - \text{ZeroDigPoint}|$$
  $$\text{If } d > 180^\circ \implies d = 360^\circ - d$$
  $$\text{Dig Bala} = \frac{180^\circ - d}{3} \quad (\text{Range: } 0 \text{ to } 60 \text{ Virupas})$$

---

### III. Kala Bala (काल बल — Temporal Strength)
$$\text{Kala Bala} = \text{Nathonnatha} + \text{Paksha} + \text{Tribhaga} + \text{Varsha-Masa-Dina-Hora} + \text{Ayana} + \text{Yuddha}$$

1. **Nathonnatha Bala (नतोन्नत बल — Diurnal / Nocturnal Strength, Max 60 Virupas):**
   - Sun, Jupiter, Venus: Strong during Day ($60 \text{ Virupas}$ at solar noon, decreasing to 0 at midnight).
   - Moon, Mars, Saturn: Strong at Night ($60 \text{ Virupas}$ at midnight, decreasing to 0 at noon).
   - Mercury: Always receives $60 \text{ Virupas}$ regardless of day or night.

2. **Paksha Bala (पक्ष बल — Fortnight Strength, Max 60 Virupas):**
   - Angle between Moon and Sun: $\theta = (\lambda_{\text{Moon}} - \lambda_{\text{Sun}} + 360^\circ) \pmod{360^\circ}$
   - For Moon: $\text{Paksha Bala} = \frac{\theta}{3}$ if $\theta \le 180^\circ$ else $\frac{360^\circ - \theta}{3} \times 2$.
   - Benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury): Receive Moon's Paksha Bala.
   - Malefics (Sun, Mars, Saturn): Receive $60 - \text{PakshaBala}_{\text{Benefics}}$.

3. **Tribhaga Bala (त्रिभाग बल — 3-Part Day/Night Strength):**
   - Day is divided into 3 equal parts (Mercury $\to$ Sun $\to$ Saturn) $\implies 60 \text{ Virupas}$.
   - Night is divided into 3 equal parts (Moon $\to$ Venus $\to$ Mars) $\implies 60 \text{ Virupas}$.
   - Jupiter always receives $60 \text{ Virupas}$.

4. **Varsha, Masa, Dina, Hora Balas (वर्ष-मास-दिन-होरा बल):**
   - Lord of Year (Varsha Adhipati) = $15 \text{ Virupas}$
   - Lord of Month (Masa Adhipati) = $30 \text{ Virupas}$
   - Lord of Day (Vara Adhipati) = $45 \text{ Virupas}$
   - Lord of Planetary Hour (Hora Adhipati) = $60 \text{ Virupas}$

5. **Ayana Bala (अयन बल — Declination Strength):**
   - Calculated from planet's declination ($\delta$) and north/south celestial motion (Uttarayana vs Dakshinayana).

---

### IV. Cheshta Bala (चेष्टा बल — Motional Strength, Max 60 Virupas)
- Measures the speed and retrograde motion of planets (Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn):
  - **Vakra (Retrograde)**: $60 \text{ Virupas}$
  - **Anuvakra (Stationary before Direct/Retro)**: $30 \text{ Virupas}$
  - **Kutila (Transitional direct)**: $30 \text{ Virupas}$
  - **Manda (Slow direct)**: $15 \text{ Virupas}$
  - **Sheeghra (Fast direct)**: $45 \text{ Virupas}$
- For Sun, Cheshta Bala equals its Ayana Bala. For Moon, Cheshta Bala equals its Paksha Bala.

### VI. Drik Bala (दृग्बल — Aspect Strength)
Measures the net benefic and malefic planetary aspects (Vedic Drishti) received by each planet from all other planets.

## 3. Minimum Required Strength (षड्बल न्यूनतम आवश्यकता)

A planet is considered **Balavan (बलवान — Fully Strong & Auspicious)** if its total Shadbala Pinda meets or exceeds the classical minimum threshold in Rupas:

| Planet | Minimum Required Rupas | Minimum Required Virupas |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sun (सूर्य)** | $6.50 \text{ Rupas}$ | $390 \text{ Virupas}$ |
| **Moon (चन्द्र)** | $6.00 \text{ Rupas}$ | $360 \text{ Virupas}$ |
| **Mars (मंगल)** | $5.00 \text{ Rupas}$ | $300 \text{ Virupas}$ |
| **Mercury (बुध)** | $7.00 \text{ Rupas}$ | $420 \text{ Virupas}$ |
| **Jupiter (गुरु)** | $6.50 \text{ Rupas}$ | $390 \text{ Virupas}$ |
| **Venus (शुक्र)** | $5.50 \text{ Rupas}$ | $330 \text{ Virupas}$ |
| **Saturn (शनि)** | $5.00 \text{ Rupas}$ | $300 \text{ Virupas}$ |

### Shadbala Ratio (बल अनुपात):
$$\text{Shadbala Ratio} = \frac{\text{Actual Virupas}}{\text{Required Virupas}}$$
- $\text{Ratio} \ge 1.0 \implies$ **Strong / Capable of Delivering Auspicious Fruit (बलवान)**
- $\text{Ratio} < 1.0 \implies$ **Deficient / Requires Upaya & Remedial Support (निर्बल)**
