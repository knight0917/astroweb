# Classical Parashari Bhava Bala (भावबल) Mathematical Reference & Formulas
**Source Text Reference:** *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (BPHS), Chapter 28: Bhavabaladhyaya (भावबलाध्याय)*

Bhava Bala (भावबल) is the mathematical system in Vedic Astrology that computes the potency and strength of all **12 Houses (Bhavas)**. While Shadbala evaluates the Grahas, Bhava Bala evaluates the capacity of each life department (health, wealth, siblings, home, children, career, marriage, etc.) to bear fruitful results.

---

## 1. Units of Measurement
- **1 Rupa (रूप)** = $60 \text{ Virupas (षष्ट्यंश / विरूपा)}$
- Total Bhava Bala is measured in **Virupas** and converted to **Rupas**.

---

## 2. The Core Components of Bhava Bala (भावबल के अंग)

$$\text{Total Bhava Bala} = \text{Bhavaadhipati Bala} + \text{Bhava Dig Bala} + \text{Bhava Drishti Bala} + \text{Bhava Dina-Ratri Bala}$$

### I. Bhavaadhipati Bala (भावाधिपति बल — Strength of the House Lord)
The lord of the sign occupying the house cusp confers its own strength upon the house.
$$\text{Bhavaadhipati Bala} = \text{Shadbala of House Lord (in Virupas)}$$

---

### II. Bhava Dig Bala (भाव दिग्बल — Directional Strength of House Signs, Max 60 Virupas)
Each sign belongs to a specific biological/symbolic classification with peak directional strength in a particular cardinal house:

1. **Nara Rashis (नर राशि — Human/Biped):**
   - Signs: Gemini (2), Virgo (5), Libra (6), 1st half of Sagittarius ($0^\circ-15^\circ$), Aquarius (10).
   - Peak Strength in **East / 1st House (Lagna)** $\implies 60 \text{ Virupas}$.
2. **Chatushpada Rashis (चतुष्पद राशि — Quadruped/Animal):**
   - Signs: Aries (0), Taurus (1), Leo (4), 2nd half of Sagittarius ($15^\circ-30^\circ$), 1st half of Capricorn ($0^\circ-15^\circ$).
   - Peak Strength in **South / 10th House (Midheaven)** $\implies 60 \text{ Virupas}$.
3. **Jalachara Rashis (जलचर राशि — Watery/Aquatic):**
   - Signs: Cancer (3), Pisces (11), 2nd half of Capricorn ($15^\circ-30^\circ$).
   - Peak Strength in **North / 4th House (Nadir)** $\implies 60 \text{ Virupas}$.
4. **Keeta Rashis (कीट राशि — Insect/Scorpion):**
   - Signs: Scorpio (7).
   - Peak Strength in **West / 7th House (Descendant)** $\implies 60 \text{ Virupas}$.

**Formula:**
$$\text{Angular Distance } d = |\text{HouseNumber} - \text{ZeroDigHouse}| \times 30^\circ$$
$$\text{If } d > 180^\circ \implies d = 360^\circ - d$$
$$\text{Bhava Dig Bala} = \frac{180^\circ - d}{3} \quad (\text{Range: } 0 \text{ to } 60 \text{ Virupas})$$

---

### III. Bhava Drishti Bala (भाव दृष्टि बल — Aspectual Strength on the House)
Measures the net influence of planetary aspects cast directly onto the house:
- Benefic planets (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, bright Moon) casting aspects add positive potency ($+15$ to $+30$ Virupas).
- Malefic planets (Saturn, Mars, Sun, Rahu, Ketu) casting aspects reduce potency ($-10$ to $-20$ Virupas).
- If the House Lord aspects its own house, an additional **$30 \text{ Virupas}$** is added (Swakshetra Drishti).

---

### IV. Bhava Dina-Ratri Bala (भाव दिन-रात्रि बल — Rising Sign Diurnal/Nocturnal Strength, Max 60 Virupas)
- **Shirshodaya Signs (शीर्षोदय — Head Rising):** Gemini (2), Leo (4), Virgo (5), Libra (6), Scorpio (7), Aquarius (10) $\implies 60 \text{ Virupas}$ during Day, $15 \text{ Virupas}$ at Night.
- **Prishtodaya Signs (पृष्ठोदय — Back Rising):** Aries (0), Taurus (1), Cancer (3), Sagittarius (8), Capricorn (9) $\implies 60 \text{ Virupas}$ at Night, $15 \text{ Virupas}$ during Day.
- **Ubhayodaya Sign (उभयोदय — Both):** Pisces (11) $\implies 60 \text{ Virupas}$ at Sunrise/Sunset sandhi, $30 \text{ Virupas}$ elsewhere.

---

## 3. Parashari Minimum Required Rupas for Houses (भाव न्यूनतम आवश्यकता)

According to Sage Parashara, the minimum strength required for a house to be classified as **Balavan (बलवान — Strong)** depends on its angular placement from Lagna:

| House Category | Houses Included | Minimum Required Rupas | Minimum Required Virupas |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kendra (केन्द्र — Angles)** | 1st, 4th, 7th, 10th | **$6.00 \text{ Rupas}$** | **$360 \text{ Virupas}$** |
| **Panapara (पणफर — Succedent)** | 2nd, 5th, 8th, 11th | **$5.50 \text{ Rupas}$** | **$330 \text{ Virupas}$** |
| **Apoklima (आपोक्लिम — Cadent)** | 3rd, 6th, 9th, 12th | **$5.00 \text{ Rupas}$** | **$300 \text{ Virupas}$** |

### Bhava Bala Ratio:
$$\text{Bhava Ratio} = \frac{\text{Actual Bhava Rupas}}{\text{Required Bhava Rupas}}$$
- $\text{Ratio} \ge 1.0 \implies$ **Strong & Fruitful House (शुभ/बलवान भाव)**
- $\text{Ratio} < 1.0 \implies$ **Weak House / Vulnerable Area (निर्बल भाव)**
