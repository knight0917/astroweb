## 1. Executive Summary & Foundational Paradigm

Maharshi Jaimini (the illustrious disciple of Sage Vedavyasa and author of *Purva Mimamsa*) revealed a profound, non-deterministic system of astrology known as **Jaimini Upadesha Sutras**.

Unlike the planetary-orbital focus of Parashari Jyotish, Jaimini operates on:
1. **Chara Karakas (Variable Planetary Significators)**: Planets change their roles in each birth chart strictly based on degrees within signs.
2. **Rashi Drishti (Sign-to-Sign Aspects)**: Signs aspect other signs directly, independent of planet locations.
3. **The 12 Arudha Padas (Reflected Maya / Material Manifestation)**: The perceived reality and societal projection of every house.
4. **Argala & Virodhargala (Intervention & Obstruction Mechanics)**: Decisive cosmic catalytic forces that open or close doors of destiny.
5. **Karakamsha & Ishta Devata (The Soul's Eternal Blueprint)**: Navamsha placement of the Atmakaraka determining spiritual liberation and genius.
6. **Jaimini Chara Dasha**: The sign-based temporal timeline of major life epochs.

## 4. The 12 Bhava Arudhas & Special Exception Rules (आरूढ़ पद)

The Arudha of a house is its **reflected material image** in society.

### Mathematical Formula:
$$\text{Distance } D = (\text{House Lord Sign} - \text{House Sign}) \pmod{12}$$
$$\text{Raw Arudha} = (\text{House Lord Sign} + D) \pmod{12}$$

### Jaimini / Parashari Classical Exception Rules (*Swasthe Dareshu...*):
1. **If Raw Arudha falls in the 1st house from the target house** $\rightarrow$ It moves **10 signs forward** (i.e. to the **10th house**).
2. **If Raw Arudha falls in the 7th house from the target house** $\rightarrow$ It moves **10 signs forward from the 7th** (i.e. to the **4th house**).

### Primary Arudha Significators:
- **Arudha Lagna (AL / A1)**: Manifested public reputation, financial standing, social stature.
- **Upapada Lagna (UL / A12)**: Marriage partner profile, marital longevity, wealth and virtue of spouse.
- **Dara Pada (A7)**: Commercial business partners, romantic relations, public transactions.
- **Rajya Pada (A10)**: Executive professional authority and career eminence.

## 5. Jaimini Argala & Virodhargala (Intervention Mechanics)

- **Primary Argala**:
  - **2nd House from Sign**: Intervenes positively $\leftrightarrow$ Obstructed by **12th House** (*Virodhargala*).
  - **4th House from Sign**: Intervenes positively $\leftrightarrow$ Obstructed by **10th House** (*Virodhargala*).
  - **11th House from Sign**: Intervenes positively $\leftrightarrow$ Obstructed by **3rd House** (*Virodhargala*).
- **Secondary Argala**:
  - **5th House from Sign**: Unlocks creative merit $\leftrightarrow$ Obstructed by **9th House** (*Virodhargala*).
- *Shubha vs. Papa Argala*: Benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury) in Argala houses offer effortless prosperity; Malefics (Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu) impose forced karmic adjustments.
