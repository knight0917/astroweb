## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy of Bhrigu Nadi

The **Bhrigu Nandi Nadi (BNN)** system, popularized by the late maestro **Sri R.G. Rao**, represents an ancient, ultra-practical school of Vedic astrology. Unlike Parashari systems that prioritize the Ascendant (Lagna) and Bhavas, BNN decodes destiny directly through **Karakas (Planetary Significators)** and their **Directional / Trine Alignments**.

Complementing BNN is **Bhrigu Saral Paddhati (BSP)**, a precise, age-correlated predictive framework that maps how planets unlock karmic events at specific years of life.

└─────────────┘        └─────────────┘                └─────────────┘        └─────────────┘

### The 1-2-12-7 Geometric Influence Model:
1. **1st & Trines (1, 5, 9)**: Combined planetary combination (e.g., Jupiter in Aries + Saturn in Leo = **Jupiter + Saturn** combination).
2. **2nd House (The Front / Future Momentum)**: Feeds and directs the energy of the planet.
3. **12th House (The Rear / Past Baggage)**: Supports or drags the planet from behind.
4. **7th House (Direct Aspect / Dialogue)**: Interacts face-to-face with the opposite planet.
5. **Retrograde Shift**: A retrograde planet exerts 50% of its influence from the **12th house (previous sign)**.
6. **Parivartana (Interchange)**: Exchanging planets cancel debilitation and function as own-sign rulers.
