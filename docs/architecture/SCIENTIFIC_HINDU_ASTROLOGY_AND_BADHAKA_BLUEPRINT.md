## 4. Planetary Avasthas (State Potency Modifiers)

1. **Baladi Avasthas (Five Age States based on Degrees in Sign)**:
   - *Odd Signs:* 0–6° Bala (25%), 6–12° Kumara (50%), 12–18° Yuva (100%), 18–24° Vriddha (10%), 24–30° Mrita (0%).
   - *Even Signs:* 0–6° Mrita (0%), 6–12° Vriddha (10%), 12–18° Yuva (100%), 18–24° Kumara (50%), 24–30° Bala (25%).
2. **Jagradadi Avasthas (Three Alertness States)**:
   - *Jagrata (Awake - Own/Exalted sign):* 100% full conscious manifestation.
   - *Swapna (Dreaming - Friend/Neutral sign):* 50% moderate manifestation.
   - *Sushupti (Sleeping - Enemy/Debilitated sign):* Low or dormant expression.
