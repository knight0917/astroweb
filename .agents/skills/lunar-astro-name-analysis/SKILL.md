# Lunar Astro Name Vibrational Energy & Astro-Phonetics Engine

This skill guides the agent to systematically analyze any person's name (calling name / *Vyavaharika Nama*) to reveal its hidden planetary resonance, psychological dynamics, relational challenges, and predicted chart signatures without needing an immediate birth horoscope.

---

## Core Analytical Workflow (6 Steps for Any Name)

When given any name (e.g. "Aniket", "Priyanka", "Alok", "Sachin", "Rohan", "Elora", "Michael", etc.):

### Step 1: Acoustic & Varna Decomposition (Svara Jyotish)
Identify the dominant sound categories using the Classical Planetary Varna Mandala:
- **Surya (Sun) — Vowels (स्वर - A, Aa, E, I, O, U):** Solar ignition, independent spirit, sovereignty, radiant confidence, paternal alignment.
- **Chandra (Moon) — Semivowels (अन्तस्थ - Y, R, L, V):** Fluid emotional resonance, rhythm, magnetic charm, empathy, public popularity.
- **Mangala (Mars) — Gutturals (क-वर्ग - K, Kh, G, Gh):** Piercing, energetic, athletic, decisive, restless, warrior impulse.
- **Budha (Mercury) — Retroflex (ट-वर्ग - T, Th, D, Dh, N):** Intellectual adaptability, analytical speed, commercial curiosity, youthful banter.
- **Guru (Jupiter) — Dentals (त-वर्ग - T, Th, D, Dh, N) & Divine Roots:** Wisdom, quiet assimilation, moral authority, philosophical receptivity, teaching.
- **Shukra (Venus) — Palatals (च-वर्ग - Ch, J, Jh, Sh, S):** Aesthetic grace, sensuality, artistic taste, romantic idealism, luxury orientation.
- **Shani (Saturn) — Labials (प-वर्ग - P, Ph, B, Bh, M) & Grounded Sounds:** Endurance, duty, service, back-office labor, slow and solid rise, humility.
- **Rahu/Ketu — Sibilants & Aspirates (Sh, S, H) / Ascetic Roots:** Unconventional charm, mystical depth, boundary breaking, spiritual detachment.

### Step 2: Morphological Root & Syllable Tokenization
Scan the name for signature etymological prefixes, roots, and suffixes:
1. **The "Pri-" / "Pree-" Root (e.g., Priyanka, Preeti, Prerit):**
   - *Planetary Energy:* Venus + Ketu / Venus under ancestral/relational karmic binding.
   - *Pattern:* Deep romantic idealism paired with partnership friction, emotional sacrifices, and lineage relationship tests.
2. **The Shiva / Ascetic Roots (e.g., Aniket, Alok, Ishan):**
   - *"Aniket"* (Homeless / Ascetic): Sun + Mars fire; independent, rebellious under authority, restless domestic energy.
   - *"Alok"* (Vision/Light): Saturnian service undertone; disciplined work ethic, quiet perseverance.
3. **The Indra / Divine Suffixes:**
   - **`"-inder"`** (*Kulwinder, Jaswinder, Harwinder*): Sun + Jupiter sovereignty; ancestral protective shield, lineage duty.
   - **`"-preet"`** (*Manpreet, Gurpreet, Harpreet*): Guru + Moon devotion; spiritual armor, heart-centered benevolence.
4. **The Golden / Auspicious Roots (e.g., Sonal, Hemant, Swarna):**
   - Pure Jupiterian vibration; receptive learning, quiet intellectual mastery, low argumentation.
5. **The Dual Charmers (e.g., Sachin, Sameer, Rohit):**
   - Moon + Venus or Sun + Mercury blend; charismatic public appeal, but prone to relational volatility or hypersensitivity.

### Step 3: Synthesis of Planetary Frequency
- Determine the **Primary Sovereign Planet** (shapes identity and life drive).
- Determine the **Secondary Resonant Planet** (shapes emotional responses and relationships).
- Identify any **Inherent Energetic Tension** (e.g. Fire vs. Water, Ascetic vs. Householder).

### Step 4: Horoscope-Free Predictive Signatures
Based on the name alone, predict the native's likely chart placements:
- Which houses or planets are highlighted (e.g. "Likely has strong Sun-Mars connection", "Venus influenced by Saturn/Rahu", "Strong 1st or 10th Saturn").

### Step 5: Chart-Synchronous Audit (If Horoscope is Available)
If birth details are provided:
- Cross-reference the calling name with the native's **Lagna Lord**, **Moon Nakshatra**, and **7th House**.
- Diagnose whether the name acts as a **Harmonizer**, **Spiritual Catalyst**, or **Friction Magnifier**.

### Step 6: Prescribe Classical & Botanical Remedies
- Suggest balancing practices: e.g. **Gayatri Mantra** at Brahma Muhurta (4:30 AM) for solar clarity, or **Kadali Vriksha** (planting/nurturing a Banana Tree) for Jupiterian harmony and progeny.
